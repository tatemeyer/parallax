# Taste

The design lens's house grammar: what this project is deliberately
going for, what it will never compromise on, and — critically — what
looks wrong but is not.

## Aesthetic intent

What look and feel this project is going for, in a sentence or two.

## Non-negotiables

Rules the design lens should treat as hard requirements, not taste.

## Deliberate violations of generic UI norms

**This is the most important section — without it the design lens
relitigates the entire aesthetic every run.** List anything that
looks like a mistake by generic UI standards but is this project's
deliberate choice, so the lens does not flag it as a defect.

## Explicitly still open to critique

What the design lens should keep scrutinizing even after the above —
areas not yet settled, or settled but still wanted under review.
